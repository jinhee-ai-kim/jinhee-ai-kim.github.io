## About the Game

**Tacoman** is a fun and chaotic taco-catching game.  
The goal is simple: **eat as many tacos as you can and reach 1000 points**.

But it is not just about grabbing everything on the screen.

- Regular tacos fall from the sky, and eating them gives you points.
- If you drink a **milkshake**, a taco rush begins and lots of tacos start falling at once.
- Sometimes, a **giant bonus taco** appears for an extra reward.
- Watch out for **spiders** and **snakes**. They may look cute, but you should never eat them.

---

## How to Play

Move your character and catch the falling tacos while avoiding dangerous creatures.

### Your mission
- Eat tacos
- Grab bonus items
- Avoid spiders and snakes
- Reach **1000 points**
